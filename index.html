The plan

A 12-month off-page roadmap

Four things are true at once: the profile is too small, too low-authority, slightly over-optimised on anchors, and unusually well-behaved on quality signals. The roadmap below is sequenced to fix the risk first, then spend the rest of the year buying real authority — not more of what already accounts for 80% of the current profile.

  1. 01

    Stop the bleeding Months 0–3

    62.5% of anchor text pointing at flooringsurgeons.co.uk is keyword-rich, against a field average of 45% — and it's paired with the weakest domain authority in the set. That combination reads as manufactured, not earned. Rebalance new link acquisition toward branded and natural anchors before adding a single new keyword-anchored link.

    • Freeze keyword-anchor requests in any outreach or guest-post brief still in progress.
    • Claim the highest-authority easy wins already in reach: expand the yell.com and thomsonlocal.com listings, complete a crunchbase.com company profile (DR 91), and fill out full descriptions everywhere a profile exists but is thin.
    • Get a person into forums.moneysavingexpert.com (DR 84) and diynot.com (DR 44) — both are active UK home-improvement communities where 11 of 12 competitors already have a foothold and flooringsurgeons.co.uk has none. Answer real threads; don’t drop links.
  2. 02

    Earn real authority Months 3–6

    You won’t out-directory your way to DR 27. You need a small number of link-worthy assets — calculators, buying guides, room-visualiser tools, cost breakdowns — that editorial and niche sites choose to cite.

    • Pitch one data-backed asset (e.g. a UK flooring cost index or fitting-time calculator) to factmags.com (DR 74) and sitelike.org (DR 66), both already linking to 10–11 competitors.
    • Guest-post on niche interiors blogs in the same neighbourhood as abeautifulspace.co.uk (DR 36) — small audience, on-topic, and genuinely followable.
    • Ignore the bulk directory sites in the opportunity list below (*directory.com, *hub*.com pattern). They inflate competitor counts but carry little real authority and won’t move DR.
  3. 03

    Close the gap Months 6–12

    With the anchor profile rebalanced and a content asset or two in market, shift to sustained, volume-focused outreach. The target isn’t matching the leader — it’s reaching the middle of the pack, where most of the field already sits.

    • Target: 580 → 900+ referring domains and DR 8.3 → 18–20 within 12 months, prioritising domains rated DR 30+.
    • Bring keyword-rich anchors from 62.5% down toward 45%, in line with the healthier end of the field (flooringsuperstore.com, woodfloorwarehouse.co.uk).
    • Re-run this audit monthly against fresh Ahrefs exports to track the gap closing, not just link count.
  4. ∞

    Protect what’s already working Ongoing

    Dofollow share, spam rate and link retention are all better than the field average right now. Don’t trade that discipline for speed — the same low-quality directories inflating competitors’ domain counts are exactly what’s pushing their spam rates toward 30–49%.
